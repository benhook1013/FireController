# Per-PR review rounds

## Implementation status

The native service, bound CLI and MCP implement explicit per-PR/channel additional-round grants, minimum/exact requirements, taper, closure/reopening and persistent native completion accounting. Source preparation and final admission recheck the grant independently of captured coverage; policy/readiness and browser assessment reads show outstanding round requirements. Backup validation preserves the control history and native completion links. Focused checks are recorded below. The service owns channel-front selection for one explicit start command. Prepared operational adoption and the offline source-helper adapter implement the selected carry boundary; acquiring a complete canonical source frame and real-project adoption remain separate steps. A grant alone performs no provider action and does not weaken the separate [review policy](reviews.md#project-policy).

## Operational controls

Hosted and CLI have independent per-PR scheduling state and independently advance from the front of the review queue. A cooldown or active review in one channel does not hold the other channel. Maximum additional rounds are counted from the request that grants them; minimum and exact-round requests remain available. A maximum permits early taper rather than requiring every allowed round. An exact-round request is explicit about the required number.

Default early taper is one eligible hosted round with zero accepted findings, or three consecutive eligible CLI rounds with zero accepted findings. A selected minimum still applies. A useful accepted correction resets the applicable dry streak; failed, incomplete, ambiguous and rate-limited attempts cannot supply successful dry rounds. Preserve the distinction between exhausted allowance, taper completion and explicit closure.

Closing a channel affects only that PR/channel. Reopening explicitly grants a new allowance. Record actor attribution and policy revision; retain consumed history when settings change. No implicit project-wide closure or reopening follows from a local change.

Head or parent movement preserves allowance history but cannot grant old capture coverage for changed code without proven applicability. Closing, tapering or exhausting a channel does not erase findings, satisfy accepted/routed obligations, establish current CI or authorize a merge. Current comparison coverage and observed merge readiness remain separate assessments.

## Native control semantics

A per-PR/channel grant is explicit administrator configuration, separate from project-wide required channels. Its minimum and maximum count additional eligible native completions from operations accepted under that grant. The maximum is between 1 and 1,000, the minimum is between zero and the maximum, and an exact request sets both to the same positive number. Defaults for a new grant are minimum zero and a taper of one hosted or three CLI dry rounds. A new grant requires an explicit maximum. Projects without a grant retain the existing single-comparison admission behavior; importing or configuring the selected controls is a deliberate adoption step.

One eligible completion has a completed native operation/attempt, validated attributable complete capture and finalized source counts. An operation counts once, independently of whether its accepted findings have been fixed. Original finalized accepted counts determine the historical dry streak; later decision/fix corrections remain separate obligations and do not rewrite consumed rounds. Failed, rate-limited and incomplete attempts consume no completed round. Queued, preparing, posting, active and uncertain requests occupy grant capacity until proven terminal; uncertain work never becomes a free retry.

Discovery completes when the maximum is consumed, or the minimum is reached and the applicable dry streak reaches its taper threshold. Exact requests therefore require the selected additional number even if taper would otherwise finish early. Before completion, an eligible round with accepted findings resets the dry streak. Reached completion remains latched until an explicit new grant; head movement and later corrections cannot silently reopen it.

Close is a revision-guarded change to that PR/channel alone, preserving its grant and consumed history. It stops new dispatch, including queued/prepared actions at their final guard, and does not cancel a possibly posted action. A posted result can still be captured, adjudicated and counted against its original grant. Reopening or changing a grant creates a fresh additional allowance and dry streak, while retaining prior grant/completion records. A new grant refuses while any operation in that PR/channel is queued, preparing, posting, active or uncertain; finish or safely settle it first. Operations accepted under an earlier grant cannot consume a later grant even if their source counts are finalized later.

Per-PR changes use their own revision and actor attribution rather than changing the project policy revision or holding unrelated PRs. Requests bind the applicable grant at acceptance and recheck it before preparation and provider dispatch. Both source preparation and final transactional admission allow another round on a covered comparison only while the bound grant still requires discovery. They preserve pending-capture/finding obligations, source identity, current authority, provider limits and reservations. For stacked progression, an explicitly finished grant replaces the predecessor single-capture requirement; outstanding evidence/fix obligations still hold dependent work. Closure or exhausted allowance without current applicable coverage remains a separate readiness limitation and never manufactures captured coverage.

Control reads are local, compact and provider-free. Administrator mutations use the ordinary authorization, expected revision, idempotency and atomic event/receipt rules. Their compact change events identify the affected PR/channel resource and its revision; the durable event cursor remains the delivery deduplication identity. The explicit run selection below retains caller authority, rechecks current authority and provider hold, and selects each channel independently. Storing a grant does not authorize provider calls. See [prepared operational adoption](#prepared-operational-adoption) for the implemented conversion boundary; acquiring a canonical source frame and qualifying a real-project adoption remain separate steps.

## Bound commands and API

Commands use the selected private administrator profile; ordinary worker credentials cannot configure grants. Reads retain the ordinary five-second budget and perform no provider work. `--min` defaults to zero; `--exact` is exclusive with `--min` and `--max`. A close requires the current revision and omits allocation fields.

```sh
fc reviews rounds read --pr 42 --channel cli
fc reviews rounds grant --pr 42 --channel cli --expected-revision 0 --max 8 --key cli-42-grant
fc reviews rounds grant --pr 42 --channel hosted --expected-revision 0 --exact 2 --key hosted-42-grant
fc reviews rounds close --pr 42 --channel cli --expected-revision 1 --key cli-42-close
```

`GET /api/v1/projects/{project_id}/reviews/rounds/{pull_number}/{channel}` returns the compact configured state, revision/epoch, bounds, consumed/dry counts, taper and closure. An unconfigured read explicitly returns `configured:false` with revision zero. Administrator `POST` at the same route accepts `expected_revision`, `action` (`grant` or `close`), and grant-only `minimum`/`maximum`, with the explicit idempotency header. The receipt contains only pull number, channel, revision, epoch and outcome. Exact-key replay retains that result after later changes or restart, with current administrator authorization still required.

MCP `review_rounds_read` and administrator `review_rounds_configure` use the shared typed client, bound project/worker and strict declared arguments. MCP grant arguments use explicit minimum and maximum; exact means equal values. Neither tool refreshes source, starts a review or silently generates a key. A dispatched write with missing or invalid output is uncertain: retain its original binding, request and key for exact recovery.

## Focused proof

Real SQLite/domain normal and race tests cover grant baselines, close/reopen isolation, current authority and demotion, CAS/replay, all live/uncertain states, capacity, minimum/taper, original finalized counts, late old-epoch completion and atomic failure. An actual ledger-write failure rolls back native finalization and receipt; exact-key retry counts once. Shared client/CLI checks use actual service handlers for strict scope, lost-response/stdout recovery and no-provider effects. Official SDK stdio checks cover the two tools, administrator enforcement, replay and complete documented discovery.

The real local runner with a synthetic executable completed three additional CLI rounds on an already covered head and stopped at the default dry taper. Closing after preparation prevented another review process from starting. Focused race proof passed in 6.945 seconds; this is controlled execution, not live provider proof. Independent source review found a missing grant check inside transactional attempt admission; that check is now present, with a distinct close-between-preparation-and-admission regression. Current captured coverage, finished discovery and observed readiness remain separately tested.

Real backup creation/inspection/inactive restore covers a closed consumed grant, a new grant unaffected by late old-epoch completion, finalized accepted findings whose fix obligations remain pending, and genuine pre-grant captures that gain no additional credit. Resealed logical, key/type, audit, progress and schema/index corruption is refused before restore writes; older bundles without the optional domain remain supported. This validates the retained authority graph and corruption boundaries, not coherent rewriting of every authoritative native record. Operational migration and installed cutover are separate boundaries.

Actual packaged `fc`, `fcd` and official-SDK stdio `fcmcp` checks completed 23 CLI invocations and seven MCP calls in a 341 ms local workload. They exercised default/exact grants, cross-client original-key replay, PR/channel/project isolation, same-session credential demotion, uncertain stdout recovery and actual daemon restart. The workload made zero external/provider calls, created no review operations or completion credit, preserved unrelated table contents and left no owned process running. This is local functional proof, not sustained throughput, live provider, native-platform or installed-service proof. The canonical `make smoke` target includes this check.

## One explicit channel-front run

One `reviews start` invocation authorizes selection and at most one review in the requested channel. The service selects the earliest unfinished target in the configured order for that channel. A blocked front is a visible hold; another channel selects independently. A closed, tapered or exhausted grant can finish discovery while pending evidence/fix obligations still hold progression. Fresh verified merged members can be skipped. A closed unmerged PR without a deliberate finished discovery policy is a hold. An optional expected PR asserts the selection and never redirects it.

The explicit run is accepted locally as a durable finite selection request before provider reads. Acceptance binds the current execution configuration, policy and installed capability plus the authenticated actor and worker. Optional expected configuration revision is a caller assertion; omission pins the configuration at acceptance. Paused execution returns `reviews_paused`; an enabled service without the requested channel returns `review_not_configured`, consistently with exact-target admission. Selection performs bounded source reads outside HTTP and database transactions. It reads only the necessary queue prefix and comparison dependencies, coalescing repeated reads within that phase, and stops once the target is established. It does not fetch unrelated reviews, CI, historical provider captures or the queue suffix.

Transient local storage overload retries only the affected local admission, claim or completion phase with cancellable backoff from 100 ms to one second. Each transaction retains its existing bound. After source facts have been acquired, retries preserve those facts and recheck current authority and bindings before completion; they never repeat provider reads or a review action. Cancellation joins the workers promptly. Non-transient persistence failures remain fatal and pending selection recovery remains explicit at restart.

Selection rechecks current caller/worker authority, configuration, policy, capability, round state and unresolved native actions before creating one ordinary exact-head review operation. That insertion, the selection-to-operation link and compact metadata event commit atomically through shared native admission. A moved queue/configuration or expected-PR mismatch retains a hold without choosing a different target. Existing exact-target requests remain available and retain their current semantics. Preparation and final dispatch retain their independent identity, allowance and authorization checks.

Original-key replay returns the original selection identity and eventual operation link after restart or later queue movement. A lost response cannot select a second target. Repeated worker wakeups and read-only selection recovery create at most one linked review operation. Held/failed selection and completed reviews require a new explicit run to request another review. Uncertain native actions retain their reservations and established recovery path. Provider-disabled or restored-held services perform no selection provider work; explicit provider resumption abandons retained pending selections along with other pending provider work.

Run calls and reads share the established short wait, explicit longer wait and immediate-return options. CLI/API result waits allow 0–900 seconds; MCP `review_start` and `review_selection_read` use 0–50 seconds to leave room below a host deadline. A pending result returns the original compact selection receipt; a useful completed result includes the selected review operation or a terminal selection hold/empty result. One bounded wait follows selection into its linked review operation when available, without forcing a separate agent polling loop. Wait preferences never change action identity, and cancellation detaches collection without canceling or repeating an admitted action. Selection and operation waits share bounded notification capacity and authorize every retained read.

## Migration boundary and proof

Carry effective current round limits, minimum/exact requirements, taper state and channel closure into the new operational policy with explicit provenance. Retain original records privately; rebuilding commands for retrospective allocation/credit-period manipulation is outside the adoption requirement. A converter must report unsupported live behavior rather than silently substitute a different rule.

Map source behavior tests to functional outcomes before implementation: additional allowance baselines, minimum/exact requests, eligible dry streaks and reset, closure/reopen isolation, independent queue advancement, head/parent movement, exhausted-versus-taper status and outstanding findings. Test observable scheduling and evidence results rather than copying legacy storage or command internals. Imported historical settings must not manufacture native captured review credit.

## Prepared operational adoption

An administrator can adopt one explicitly selected effective PR/channel rule through a prepared native preview/apply operation. The prepared record carries remaining minimum and maximum, initial dry streak, taper threshold, reached taper and explicit closure, together with repository identity, source identity/key and source digest. It is configuration provenance, never a captured review or completed native round. Native `used` begins at zero. A carried CLI dry streak of two therefore needs one further eligible zero-accepted round to reach the usual threshold of three; a later accepted native round resets that streak normally.

Remaining maximum zero means already exhausted and does not grant a replacement round. Reached taper is sticky even if the source's current dry counter is lower after later observations. A carried reached threshold still waits for any remaining required minimum; later accepted rounds can reset the dry counter without erasing that reached threshold. The carried threshold is explicit; ordinary new grants still use the default channel threshold and clear the carried initial state. Closure, taper and exhaustion remain separate facts. Import does not satisfy current coverage, pending findings, CI or merge readiness.

Adoption initializes an unconfigured destination PR/channel only. Preview binds the normalized record to the destination instance/project, current administrator/worker and expected control revision zero. Apply uses an explicit idempotency key and rechecks repository identity, configuration/queue/channel, current authority and absence of unresolved native operations for that PR/channel before committing control, original baseline, provenance, receipt and one compact round-setting event atomically. Same-key exact replay returns the original receipt after later native activity; changed input refuses. A different key cannot overwrite an adopted or subsequently edited control. Ordinary grants and close retain their existing authority and replay rules.

The native prepared record accepts bounded remaining limits up to 1,000, a positive threshold up to 1,000, and an initial dry counter up to 1,000. Unsupported unbounded or larger source rules require an explicit operator decision rather than a substituted cap or reset. A source-specific helper must obtain effective counts and taper from the canonical source projection, require authoritative source clearance of accepted findings and incoming/outgoing routes, refuse active/ambiguous or unchecked evidence, preserve its private input and digest, and report unsupported source behavior. Imported display history does not create native execution holds; unknown or unfinished source obligations must therefore refuse operational adoption even when the source allowance is exhausted, tapered or closed. A sealed database and raw review-row counts alone do not establish that projection. Source export and conversion belong to optional tooling; the service accepts only the generic prepared record.

The bound administrator commands use an owner-private prepared file of at most 16 KiB:

```sh
fc reviews rounds adopt preview --file /private/import/control.json
fc reviews rounds adopt apply --file /private/import/control.json --preview SHA256 --key selected-control
```

These are setup commands rather than worker MCP mutations. The [optional source adapter guide](../tools/legacyimport/README.md#prepare-effective-review-controls) owns source input requirements and refusal boundaries.

Backup validation checks both the original carried baseline and subsequent genuine native completions, including ordinary closure and replacement grants. Upgrading a version-one round domain preserves its original rows/history and zero initial carry. The imported baseline never inserts native attempts, captures or completion rows. Focused native HTTP/client/CLI and backup tests cover carried accounting, guarded replay, version-one upgrade and corruption refusal. The optional source adapter has synthetic proof against explicitly selected copied helpers; the combined packaged control/message workload results, source checkpoints and qualification limits are recorded in the [adoption proof](legacy-adoption.md#executed-message-and-operational-adoption-proof).

## Explicit selection proof

Focused real SQLite/worker, HTTP, typed CLI/client and official-SDK stdio checks cover one selection-to-operation link, original-key replay after restart/configuration changes, expected-PR refusal, current authority, grant changes, pending evidence, channel independence and atomic failure. Selection/result notification waits share the ordinary bounded wait capacity; a one-second HTTP regression follows delayed selection into the still-pending child within that same budget. The current 50-second maximum selected MCP wait remains pending beyond the ordinary call budget while another tool completes, and cancellation leaves durable work unchanged. The full adapter race suite passed in 55.928 seconds after aligning discovery, argument validation and the SDK fixtures to this limit.

Independent source review found that receiving routed findings could be bypassed after a front had captured coverage or finished rounds. Selection now checks those independent obligations both when classifying source and in the final link transaction. Genuine route/capture regressions fail without the guard and pass with it, including an incoming route created while source selection is in flight.

The first actual packaged Linux workload completed in 302 ms, with seven-ms enqueue, eight CLI invocations, two SDK MCP calls and exactly eight GitHub-shaped GETs tunneled only to an owned loopback HTTPS fixture. The packaged daemon selected the expected target past a closed grant and created one ordinary review operation, which deliberately held at a trusted failing Git stub before allowance or review execution. Expected-PR refusal, lost stdout, replay, later queue changes and daemon restart preserved the original link and source-call counts. No CLI allowance/review executable ran, no captured credit was created, input and unrelated-table data stayed unchanged, and owned processes stopped. This proves packaged selection into native admission; it does not establish a completed live provider review, other platforms, fleet throughput or installed cutover.

Real creation, inspection and inactive restore retain selected and pending selection state and its original operation link. Resealed schema, key/type, configuration and child-link corruption is refused before target writes. Explicit provider resumption atomically abandons pending selections with an audited count; a later reconciliation failure rolls back that abandonment. A subsequent real worker decision with otherwise valid authority makes no source call and creates no child for an abandoned selection. Older bundles/releases omit the new zero selection count.
