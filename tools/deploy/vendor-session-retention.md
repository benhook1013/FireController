# CodeRabbit session retention qualification

The controller's authoritative CLI output and evidence are retained independently of the vendor's private session directory. `internal/reviewruntime/vendor_retention.go` now provides an **inactive, read-only** qualification policy for one session. It is not a collector, does not scan or delete files, and is not called by a maintenance loop.

A prospective cleanup consumer must enumerate at most a bounded batch, reject symlinks and unexpected layouts, then qualify each session with this policy. Qualification requires the version-2 completed-session marker, an exact native attempt identity in Git metadata, matching completed CLI operation/attempt, the existing capability-bound reservation/chronology checks, at least seven days since completion and evidence recording, and the exact independently retained capture hash, size and identity. Candidate and comparison-base anchors must agree. Active, failed, uncertain, recent, missing-capture and mismatched sessions remain protected. Before deletion, a consumer must revalidate all files and journal facts and hold exclusive custody against provider use. The predicate is not permission to delete.

Deleting vendor histories preserves controller findings and raw outputs, but can permanently remove vendor-only summaries, incremental context and local vendor statistics/history. These are not claimed byte-recoverable from controller captures. No compression, archive transfer, or automatic deletion has been enabled. An explicitly authorized initial cleanup must identify concrete files and loss. Future permanent deletion of nonrecoverable vendor history cannot silently rely on a blanket approval; required action-time confirmation remains applicable.

Tests cover the real synthetic runner-to-capture-to-journal path, active reservations, recent completion, invalid markers, damaged captures, anchor mismatches, capture mismatches and invalid/reversed timing. They perform no provider requests or review adjudication.
